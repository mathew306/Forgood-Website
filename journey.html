What onboarding includes

From the free consultation to your plan: assessment, food, training, and habits. All-In is coached in person in Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, and Jacksonville, with a weekly 1:1.

How coaching actually starts

Six steps. The consultation is free. The first charge posts on the day of the onboarding call, and for All-In that day starts the 3-month program.

1

Book a free consultation

We talk through the weight you want to lose, the job and family calendar you already have, and whether in-person coaching in Nocatee and nearby is the right fit. No pitch required to get a straight answer.

No charge
2

Assessment

We look at where you are: how you move, what your week actually holds, and what has gotten in the way before. The program is built from that, not from a generic template.

3

Personalized nutrition targets and a sample day

You get calorie and protein-forward macro targets set for you, plus one sample day so the first week of eating is concrete. You log daily in an app like MacroFactor. Planning stays simple. The standard is that you should never feel hungry.

4

Training plan with form videos

Strength sessions are about 30 minutes, four or five days a week. Early work is machine-first. Every exercise has a form video, and any exercise can be swapped if it does not fit your body.

5

Habit plan

Habits are placed into the routine you already run. Alongside lifting, the week includes about four hours of enjoyable, flexible movement — walks, sauna, play. Consistency beats perfection.

6

Onboarding call

The first charge posts on the day of the onboarding call. For All-In, that day starts the 3-month program. All-In is a 3-month commitment, with no mid-program cancellation. It includes a weekly in-person 1:1 and DEXA testing before and after.
