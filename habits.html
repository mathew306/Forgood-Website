The routines that make strength stick

Sleep, recovery and the small daily habits that decide whether your training actually compounds — or resets every few weeks.

Small routines, repeated on purpose

Training is only one input

Two sessions a week can't outrun five nights of bad sleep or a stress load that never lets up. So habits coaching isn't an add-on — it's how we protect the return on the training you're already doing.

We track a small number of daily basics — sleep, steps, stress, consistency — and treat them with the same structure as a lift: start where you are, add one layer at a time, and don't chase perfect.

The goal isn't a rigid routine. It's a handful of habits sturdy enough to survive a bad week.

How we build habits that last

Same weekly rhythm as training: check in, adjust, repeat.

Pick one

One habit at a time

We choose the single habit that will move the needle most right now — usually sleep — and leave everything else alone.

Check in

Weekly, not daily, pressure

A short weekly review keeps you honest without turning every day into a scorecard.

Layer up

Add the next one

Once a habit holds for a few weeks on its own, we layer in the next — recovery, stress, consistency.
