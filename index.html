For dads who are busy, and carrying extra weight

High-performing job. Full calendar. Body fat you want gone. You’re either not sure how to take it off, or you’ve already decided you don’t have time. Coaching starts from that week.

The client

Demanding work, real life

Meetings, travel, and kids already fill the week. The plan is built around that life.

The commitment

A real week, built to fit

About 30 minutes of strength, four or five days a week, plus about four hours of movement you can enjoy — a walk, the sauna, time playing with your kids. That is a real weekly commitment, scheduled so it fits a busy life.

In person

Nocatee and nearby

Coaching is in person in Nocatee and surrounding areas: Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, and Jacksonville.

Energy, and the long game

Fat loss is the main goal. Plenty of dads also want more energy through a long day, and a body that holds up over time — including blood pressure, cholesterol, and simply being around for the people who need them. Those are fair reasons to train. Coaching supports the work. Your doctor handles anything clinical. Consult your doctor before you start.

What onboarding includes

You leave with a plan that matches your week, then check-ins so it keeps matching. See the steps.

01

Assessment

Where you are now: your schedule, how you move, and what you want to change. The plan starts there, not from a template.

02

Nutrition targets and a sample day

Personalized calories and protein-forward macros, plus one sample day so planning stays simple. The standard is that you should never feel hungry.

03

Training plan with form videos

About 30-minute strength sessions, machine-first early on. Every exercise has a form video, and any exercise can be swapped.

04

Habit plan

Habits set into the routine you already run, including about four hours a week of movement you enjoy. Consistency beats perfection.

05

Follow-up check-ins

Food, training, and habits get adjusted as your actual weeks happen — travel, late nights, and all.
