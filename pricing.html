Questions people ask before starting

If yours isn't here, ask on your intro call — there's no wrong question.

Do I need to sign a contract?

Foundations and For Good run month-to-month — pause, switch, or cancel anytime with two weeks' notice, no cancellation fee. All-In is the one exception: it's a 6-month commitment, since it's built as a fast, high-touch push toward your results rather than an ongoing plan.

What happens when my 6 months are up?

You move to For Good at $199/month. Same platform, same coaching access, same community — at less than a third of the All-In price — because the goal was always to get you to a place where you can maintain your results on your own, not to keep you paying for how you got there.

What's the founding member rate?

The first 10 people to join All-In lock in $499/month instead of $999/month, for their one 6-month term. It's a launch offer, not a standing discount — once those 10 spots are filled, or a founding member's six months are up, the rate returns to $999.

What if I've never trained with a coach before?

Most clients start exactly there. Your first session is a movement assessment, not a workout — we build the plan around what your body can do today.

Is nutrition coaching included, and what's the DEXA testing about?

Nutrition guidance is built into every tier through your asynchronous coaching. All-In adds DEXA/DexaFit body composition and RMR testing at the start and end of your program, so your results are measured, not just felt.

What happens on the intro call?

Twenty minutes, no pressure and no pitch. We talk through your goals, your schedule and your training history, and recommend the tier that fits — even if that's none of them yet.
