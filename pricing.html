Questions people ask before starting

If yours isn’t here, ask on your free consultation — there’s no wrong question.

Where does coaching happen?

In person in Nocatee and surrounding areas: Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, and Jacksonville. The tier details on this page are the offer currently published, and they are marked for review above.

Do I need to sign a contract?

As currently published: Foundations and For Good run month-to-month — pause, switch, or cancel anytime with two weeks’ notice, no cancellation fee. All-In is the one exception: it’s a 6-month commitment, since it’s built as a fast, high-touch push toward your results rather than an ongoing plan.

What happens when my 6 months are up?

As currently published, you move to For Good at $199/month. Same platform, same coaching access, same community — at less than a third of the All-In price — because the goal was always to get you to a place where you can maintain your results on your own, not to keep you paying for how you got there.

What’s the founding member rate?

As currently published, the first 10 people to join All-In lock in $499/month instead of $999/month, for their one 6-month term. It’s a launch offer, not a standing discount — once those 10 spots are filled, or a founding member’s six months are up, the rate returns to $999.

What if I’ve never trained with a coach before?

Most clients start exactly there. Onboarding begins with an assessment. Early strength work is machine-first, with a form video for every exercise, and any exercise can be swapped.

Is nutrition coaching included, and what’s the DEXA testing about?

Nutrition coaching — personalized calorie and protein-forward targets, a sample day, and daily logging in an app like MacroFactor — is part of the program. As currently published, All-In also lists DEXA/DexaFit body composition and RMR testing at the start and end. That testing line is part of the published tier, which is under review with the rest of this page.

What happens on the free consultation?

A conversation, no pressure and no pitch. We talk through your goals, your schedule, and whether in-person coaching in Nocatee fits the week you actually have.

What happens after I sign up?

Onboarding covers an assessment, personalized nutrition targets and a sample day, a training plan with form videos, a habit plan, and follow-up check-ins. See onboarding step by step.
