Questions people ask before starting

If yours isn’t here, ask on your free consultation — there’s no wrong question.

Where does coaching happen?

All-In is in person in Nocatee and surrounding areas: Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, and Jacksonville. Foundations is fully virtual.

Do I need to sign a contract?

Foundations and For Good are ongoing and bill month to month. Pause, switch, or cancel with two weeks’ notice. All-In is a 3-month in-person program: $1,000 a month, or $2,500 paid upfront.

How long does each plan last?

All-In runs for 3 months. Foundations has no fixed term. For Good is $199 a month, ongoing, and is unlocked after a program.

What if I’ve never trained with a coach before?

Most clients start exactly there. Onboarding begins with an assessment. Early strength work is machine-first, with a form video for every exercise, and any exercise can be swapped.

Is nutrition coaching included?

Yes. All-In includes personalized calorie and protein-forward targets, a sample day, and daily logging in an app like MacroFactor, along with the training and habit plan.

What happens on the free consultation?

A conversation, no pressure and no pitch. We talk through your goals, your schedule, and whether in-person coaching in Nocatee fits the week you actually have.

What happens after I sign up?

Onboarding covers an assessment, personalized nutrition targets and a sample day, a training plan with form videos, a habit plan, and follow-up check-ins. See onboarding step by step.
