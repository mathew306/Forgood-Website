Coaching that fits a busy dad’s week

For Good is in-person personal training and nutrition coaching in Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, and Jacksonville. Mathew Trowbridge is the coach. The work is strength, food, and habits that fit the life you already have.

Hi, I’m Mathew Trowbridge

I didn’t get into fitness to help people live in the gym. I got into it to help people build the fitness they need to do the things they love.

I work with busy dads whose bodies are starting to hold them back from surfing, tennis, soccer, basketball, and playing with their kids. I help them reclaim those activities, feel better, and have more energy. The goal is to empower you to take control of your body through a proven approach to fitness and nutrition, so you can do more of what you love while feeling your best.

My Approach:
Structure Meets Adaptability

Too many programs demand that you reshape your life around a rigid routine. Your program should adapt to your life, and be simple enough to follow for the rest of your life.

When we work together, we focus on:

Strength

Strength training that fits your schedule

Easy, repeatable workouts that get results without burning you out.

Nutrition

Nutrition planning

Training without a nutrition plan is like swimming upstream. You get a focused plan for how you fuel your body.

Habits

Sustainable habits

Real change happens when we build consistency over time through daily habit-building techniques.

Background

Currently completing NASM Certified Personal Trainer (CPT) and Certified Nutrition Coach (CNC).

My background is civil engineering. I was founder and CEO of an urgent care, and I own contrast therapy franchise locations. That work is analytical: find the root cause, then build from it. After years in the proactive wellness space, I help people escape quick-fix fitness and build long-term, functional strength that lasts for good.
