Coaching that fits a busy dad’s week

For Good is in-person personal training and nutrition coaching in Nocatee, Ponte Vedra, St. Johns, and Jacksonville. Mathew Trowbridge is the coach. The work is strength, food, and habits that fit the life you already have.

Who you’ll work with

You’ll work with Mathew Trowbridge, in person, in Nocatee and the surrounding area. The coaching is practical: a strength plan you can run on a workweek, nutrition targets you can log, and habits placed into the routine you already have.

The name is the aim. For good. The plan is meant to hold on ordinary weeks, including the ones that go sideways. Consistency beats perfection.
