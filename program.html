Strength

Short sessions, often enough to matter

Strength work is about 30 minutes, four or five days a week. That is frequent on purpose, and it is scheduled around a job that already fills the day. The sessions fit a busy life, and the week still holds a real training commitment.

Early on, sessions are machine-first so you can load the right muscles with a safer setup while the movement is still new. Every exercise comes with a form video you can rewatch. If something does not agree with your body, swap it that session.

Coaching is in person in Nocatee and surrounding areas, so form gets eyes on it, not just a video.

Nutrition

Targets you can hit without going hungry

Nutrition coaching sets personalized calorie and protein-forward macro targets. You log food daily in an app like MacroFactor, against those targets — not against a rigid menu you abandon by Thursday.

Planning stays simple: enough structure to know what “on plan” looks like, including a sample day from onboarding, and enough room for the meals you already eat with your family. The standard we hold is straightforward. You should never feel hungry.

If you are hungry, the targets are wrong for this stretch of your life, and they get adjusted at the next check-in.

Built into the week you already have

Habits

Attached to the routine you already run

Habits sit inside the day you already have — the commute, the evening, the parts of the week that already exist.

Alongside the strength sessions, the week includes about four hours of enjoyable, flexible movement. Walks, sauna, playing with your kids. Choose a form you will still pick when the week gets loud.

The rule we coach to: consistency beats perfection. A held-together week beats a flawless one you drop.
