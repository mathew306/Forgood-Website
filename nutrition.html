Simple fuel, no meal plan to fail at

High-protein, practical eating that adapts to your actual week — travel, takeout and busy nights included — instead of a rigid plan you'll abandon by week two.

Fewer rules, better defaults

Most nutrition plans fail because they ask you to be a different person than you actually are. We start from your current routine — what you already eat, when, and with whom — and adjust it, rather than replacing it wholesale.

The one rule we hold firm on is protein: enough at each meal to support the strength you're building and keep you full. Everything else — carbs, fats, timing — flexes around your life and your goals.

No foods are off-limits. We talk about how something fits into your week, not whether you're "allowed" to have it.
